## Summary

Run, jump and shoot through two levels to reach the goal. You have five health points, and touching a checkpoint lets you restart there when you die. Hold aim to stand still and shoot in any of 16 directions.

## Highlights

- Responsive movement: double jump, jump height that follows how long you hold the button, and a short grace time at ledges.
- Each game object (player, enemy, bullet, checkpoint, goal) is its own small scene, and levels are built by placing them.
- Signals (observer pattern): the player emits events like `died` and `health_changed`, and the HUD and game manager react.
- Enemies run a state machine, for example `PATROL → CHASE → DIVE → DEAD`.
- A shared `take_damage()` method lets one bullet type hit the player and every enemy.
- Checkpoints survive a level reload, and keyboard and gamepad share the same input actions.

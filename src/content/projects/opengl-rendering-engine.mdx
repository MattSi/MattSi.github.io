## Summary

The renderer is built around a component-based scene system loaded from JSON, a Blinn-Phong renderer with normal mapping, GPU-side procedural instancing, and a chain of ten screen-space post-processing effects that can be toggled and tuned live.

## Highlights

- Procedural instancing with no per-instance buffer: an integer hash of the instance ID sets position, height, rotation and scale in the vertex shader.
- The scene renders into an RGBA16F framebuffer, then ping-pong passes: fog, depth of field, edge detection, color grading, water distortion and more.
- Lighting with 1 directional, up to 8 point and 8 spot lights; diffuse, specular, emission and tangent-space normal maps.
- A reflection-style type registry creates components by class name from scene files.
- Missing licensed assets fall back to CC0 models and a procedural nebula sky, so a fresh clone still runs.

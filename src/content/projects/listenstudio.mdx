## Summary

Drop in any audio or video file to transcribe it with Whisper, then listen to it sentence by sentence. Or write your own text, hear it read aloud by a neural voice, record yourself, and compare your pronunciation with the reference sentence by sentence.

## Highlights

- Sentence splitting sets its silence threshold from each recording's noise floor.
- Transcription runs on a worker thread with pause and cancel; long recordings stream to disk instead of memory.
- Pronunciation check aligns your Whisper transcript with the text word by word and marks skipped and misread words.
- Speed change without pitch shift, and a limiter on volume boost so loud parts never clip.
- Online neural voices with an offline fallback model.
